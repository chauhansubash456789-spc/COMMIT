FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staking, security, and verification.

🛡️ How does No-Loss Staking protect my principal?

When you select No-Loss Staking, your USDC principal is deposited into a Solana lending pool (e.g., Kamino or MarginFi). The principal is programmatically protected in escrow and remains 100% yours. If you fail to meet your goal, only the accrued yield is forfeited and paid out to the verifier or community pool.

📷 How does the Solana Blockhash prevent cheating?

When submitting peer proof (like a workout photo or book reading video), the app queries the current live Solana Devnet blockhash (e.g. SOL-7Z8z9x). You must display this dynamic code on a paper or screen within the media. Because blockhashes change every ~400ms, pre-recorded photos or old footage cannot be reused.

🔑 Do I need Phantom or real SOL to test this app?

No! While Phantom and Solflare are fully supported, we also built a 1-Click Devnet Evaluator into the Connect Wallet modal (Alice, Bob, Charlie). It generates local Ed25519 signatures in your browser using tweetnacl so judges can test the entire experience in 5 seconds. You can also click "+ Airdrop" to get 100 Devnet USDC and 1 SOL instantly.

⚖️ What happens if a verifier is dishonest or denies my proof?

Commit Protocol features a formal Dispute Resolution lifecycle. If you believe an outcome was unfair, you can open a dispute. This immediately freezes all on-chain escrow settlements. A panel of administrators reviews the SHA-256 evidence hash and can overturn the result, safeguarding your stake.
